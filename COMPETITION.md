# Competition case study — FitLoop

**Programme:** Generating Genius Entrepreneurship Genius, 2026  
**Final presentation:** 5 September 2026  
**Entry:** FitLoop — a clothing alterations marketplace concept  
**Result:** Team did not win the overall competition; I received an **Outstanding Individual award**.

## Business idea

Many clothing returns happen because an item nearly fits. Our team proposed a platform where a customer could find a local tailor, explain the alteration, review quotes and track a booking instead of returning the item.

## My contribution

I took the lead on the product prototype and website experience, purchased the domain and deployed the public competition demonstration. I worked on the pitch presentation and the solution/demo, customer-acquisition and risk sections. I also presented to the judges and participated in the Q&A.

The project and pitch were created by a team; the website and product-design tasks above describe my own contribution.

## What we demonstrated

The website showed an interactive customer journey with discovery filters, alteration requests, example quotations, simulated booking progress, sample messaging and customer/tailor dashboards. The demo was designed to make the business concept tangible, not to process real orders or payments.

## Lessons from the judges

The website and pitch attracted positive feedback. More importantly, the questioning made clear that our proposed investment valuation needed a stronger basis. We hadn't completed enough financial or customer research to defend the valuation convincingly.

If I repeated the competition, I would interview local tailors and prospective customers earlier, test service pricing and transaction economics, and base the investment case on evidence rather than assumptions.
